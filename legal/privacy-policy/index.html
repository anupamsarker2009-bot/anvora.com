Who we are

Anvora is a marketing company. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data when you use our website or services.

What we collect

  • Information you give us, such as your name, email address, company and message when you fill in a form.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide our services.
  • To send our newsletter, if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand and improve how our website is used.

Sharing your data

We never sell your data. We only share it with trusted service providers who help us run our business, under appropriate agreements.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@anvora.com.
